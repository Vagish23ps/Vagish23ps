## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 01. SATYA — AI-Powered Misinformation Detection

**An approach towards evaluating the credibility of online information.**

SATYA is an AI-powered misinformation detection platform designed to analyze claims using multiple sources of evidence rather than relying on a single signal.

**What it focuses on:**

- **Claim Analysis:** Examines submitted claims to understand what is being asserted.
- **Fact-Checking Sources:** Uses available fact-checking information to identify supporting or contradicting evidence.
- **Semantic Evidence Retrieval:** Looks for contextually relevant evidence instead of relying only on exact keyword matches.
- **News Source Analysis:** Incorporates information from news sources to provide additional context.
- **Credibility Estimation:** Combines multiple signals to estimate how credible a claim may be.

**Engineering concepts:** AI integration, semantic search, information retrieval, evidence analysis, and full-stack application development.

### 02. Lectra — Personal Productivity & Organization App

**A centralized space for managing everyday academic and personal work.**

Lectra is an application I built to make it easier to capture information, organize tasks, and keep track of important deadlines.

**Key features:**

- **Quick Notes:** Capture information and organize it into structured entries.
- **Task Management:** Add and track pending work associated with entries.
- **Deadline Tracking:** Keep track of upcoming, approaching, and overdue deadlines.
- **Reminders & Notifications:** Receive reminders for daily activities, deadlines, and scheduled work.
- **File Attachments:** Attach PDFs and images to keep related resources accessible.
- **Local Storage:** Uses browser-side storage technologies to manage application data and attachments.

**Engineering concepts:** React/Next.js, TypeScript, Zustand, Capacitor, IndexedDB, Android integration, and local notifications.
